Our paper is out today in *Science Advances*! We identify *ergatoid*, a supergene that creates a new caste in the moss ant *Leptothorax AF-erg* by turning larvae that would become winged queens into wingless “ergatoid” queens. This paper represents six years of work by me, Géraldine Bienvenu, Christina Daly, and Buck Trible. We’re excited to see it out! [link](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aei5142)
